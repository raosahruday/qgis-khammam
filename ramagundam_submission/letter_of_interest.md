**Subject:** Letter of Interest (LOI) for the Implementation of "RVS Eco" Smart Municipal Cleaning & Waste Monitoring System in Ramagundam

Respected Sir/Madam,

On behalf of RVS Eco Projects, we are pleased to submit this Letter of Interest to express our keen desire to partner with the Ramagundam Municipal Corporation (RMC) in transforming the city's solid waste management and sanitation operations through our state-of-the-art GIS-linked, AI-enabled sanitation tracking platform.

Ramagundam, known as the industrial and energy hub of Telangana, presents unique challenges in sanitation management due to its high density, vast road networks, and industrial-residential layout. Traditional manual monitoring of sweeping, garbage collection, and road cleaning is often resource-intensive and prone to supervision gaps.

Our solution, **RVS Eco**, addresses these challenges by digitizing municipal operations into a centralized, transparent, and automated workflow:
1. **GIS Road & Division Mapping**: Full digital layout of all municipal wards and road networks.
2. **Jawan & Vehicle GPS Tracking**: Real-time progress monitoring of sweepers and transport vehicles.
3. **QR Code Attendance & Validation**: Mandatory scanning at physical start and end locations of assigned cleaning routes to ensure physical presence.
4. **AI automated Audits**: AI-powered computer vision models that automatically evaluate Jawan-uploaded photo proofs for cleanliness metrics, replacing manual verification bottlenecks.

We are ready to conduct a live demonstration of our platform for the Ramagundam Municipal Corporation's administrative and health officials at your convenience. Enclosed with this letter are the detailed Project Proposal, Budget Estimate, and Project Brochure for your kind review.

We look forward to an opportunity to present our proposal in person and contribute to making Ramagundam a cleaner, smarter, and healthier city.

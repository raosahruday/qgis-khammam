Privacy Policy for Khammam Cleanup

Effective Date: July 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Khammam Cleanup ("we", "us", or "our") handles personal information when you use our mobile application and associated administrative services.

1. Information We Collect

To provide municipal waste management and road cleaning coordination services, we collect the following information:

  • Location Data: We collect precise location data while you use the application to display assigned ward boundaries, map cleaning task routes, and verify task completion on municipal roads.
  • Photos & Camera Access: We request camera access to allow municipal workers and Jawans to capture proof-of-work photos before and after task execution.
  • Account & Personal Information: We store account credentials including worker name, phone number, role (Sanitary Inspector, Jawan, Commissioner), and ward assignments for authentication.

2. How We Use Information

The collected data is used exclusively to:

  • Display assigned road tasks and geographical boundaries on interactive maps.
  • Verify completion of road maintenance and sanitation assignments.
  • Enable administrative monitoring by municipal supervisors and commissioners.
  • Ensure secure login and role-based authorization.

3. Data Sharing & Security

  • No Third-Party Advertising: We do not sell, rent, or share any personal data with third-party advertisers or commercial entities.
  • Data Encryption: All data transmitted between the mobile application and server infrastructure is encrypted using HTTPS standard SSL protocols.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Work assignment records and completed task photos are retained for administrative auditing purposes. Users may request account deletion or data removal by contacting our administrative team at raosahruday5@gmail.com.
